Research direction

Questions I want to answer.

I am interested in methods that explain why outcomes happen, remain reliable outside the training distribution, and communicate uncertainty responsibly.

01 / CAUSALITY

Causal inference & uplift modeling

Estimating heterogeneous treatment effects and learning policies that optimize incremental outcomes rather than correlations alone.

02 / GENERALIZATION

Causal representation & OOD

Learning representations that capture stable mechanisms and support robust prediction when environments or populations change.

03 / RELIABILITY

Trustworthy & probabilistic AI

Building models whose assumptions, uncertainty, and failure modes can be inspected before they influence real decisions.
